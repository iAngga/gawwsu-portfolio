Hi, I'm Angga, also known as gawwsu.

By day I'm the only IT technician at a vocational high school in Bandung: eight computer labs, a Docker server full of student projects, and whatever breaks in between. I'm working my way toward DevOps and Linux system administration. By night (when I'm not asleep) I make small games and pixel art.

This site is where I put all of it in one place:

- **Projects:** infra write-ups like my [game server stack](/projects/game-server-stack/), plus the games I've made
- **Art:** pixel art, 3D props, and photos
- **Blog:** notes from tinkering, mostly in English, some casual posts in Bahasa Indonesia

The site itself is part of the learning. It's built with Astro and deployed by Cloudflare on every push. [Here's how](/blog/how-this-site-deploys/).
