A rhythm RPG made for **GIMJAM 2026**, still in development.

Aldric wakes up in a forest that feels familiar but twisted, a world built from his own fears. A mysterious figure called the Wanderer tells him his heart has been cursed and sealed. To wake up, he has to cross this nightmare and fight monsters that stand for his anxieties.

## How it plays

- **Explore:** walk around with W A S D, talk and interact with Space.
- **Fight:** pick actions like Attack or Items from a menu, then press Space when the notes hit the target zone to land the move.

How you fight shapes who Aldric becomes.
